# Remaining launch prerequisites

Updated 28 October 2026. The owner deployed the demo to Render and verified samples and the Free Preview. A Replicate account has been created and its payment-method setup was confirmed by the owner's screenshot; that screenshot showed $0 provider credit. No actual provider funding or successful external inference is inferred from it. The website remains a demonstration, with no live AI, real customer payments or bank payouts verified.

1. **Gateway eligibility and payouts:** confirm an actual provider accepts the Zambia-based operator, AI-generation product, monthly usage allowance and payout bank. Paddle is a candidate only. Its current [unsupported-country list](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle) does not list Zambia, but that is not merchant, product or bank approval. Its [AUP](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle) requires review of realistic AI generation and stored-value/credit offerings. Use a suitable alternative if it does not approve this service. Do not equate international customers with merchant payout eligibility.
2. **Real monthly billing integration:** the app now has a local monthly subscription simulation, separate paid-period allowances and cancellation. Implement and verify the chosen gateway's recurring USD prices, hosted checkout, initial/renewal payments, signed and idempotent lifecycle webhooks, authoritative state reconciliation, cancellations/customer portal, failed-payment handling, refunds/disputes and deletion that cannot leave recurring charges running. The older one-time Paddle sandbox adapter is not this integration. Run actual credentialed sandbox tests before a small authorized live test.
3. **Real AI implementation and verification:** select exact image/video models and settings, validate pricing/capabilities/terms/geography, complete model-specific mapping and worker integration, moderation, bounded private output copying and uncertain-outcome reconciliation. The generic Replicate transport is not registered with the worker; external models remain disabled and unverified. Fund the provider within an agreed testing budget and verify actual billable results. A token or $10 balance alone does not finish this work.
4. **Production identity and storage:** configure Clerk and a private S3-compatible bucket. Test verified signup/recovery/sign-out, upload/download ownership, media deletion and abuse prevention. Browser demo identities and local ephemeral media do not supply these protections. The 3+1 live trial remains off until verified models, identities and spending caps are configured; its six-second video needs compatible model support.
5. **Permanent hosting and data:** replace the temporary Render free PostgreSQL database before its expiry and provide backups/restore checks, suitable web/worker resources, durable object storage and monitoring. The free web service sleeps, so it is suitable for preview rather than a claim of always-ready commercial operation. The current combined launcher is demo-only; production needs supervised real web/worker deployment. No custom domain has been configured.
6. **Operator and customer policies:** supply actual operator/legal identity, support and abuse contacts and merchant details. Review draft monthly renewal, allowance expiry, cancellation, refund, privacy/retention and content policies against the selected gateway and applicable consumer law. Cancellation must remain accessible and must stop real future charges.
7. **Cost and operations:** measure model invoices and full-allowance exposure, payment/payout/FX fees, free-trial conversion and costs, hosting/support/acquisition, refunds and taxes. Reprice model quotes and monthly plans before selling; maintain provider hard limits plus app admission caps, audited reconciliation, cleanup/backups and incident monitoring. See `UNIT-ECONOMICS.md`.
